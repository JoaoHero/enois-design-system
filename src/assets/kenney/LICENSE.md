# Kenney assets

The sprites in this folder come from two packs by [Kenney](https://kenney.nl) and are used under
**Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)** — the license the packs ship with. Credit is not required, but
we give it anyway.

| File                                                                                | Pack              | Source                                   |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| `star-01.webp`, `star-04.webp`, `magic-05.webp`, `light-01.webp`                    | Particle Pack 1.1 | https://kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack   |
| `leaf-01.webp` … `leaf-04.webp`, `grass-01.webp`, `grass-02.webp`, `flower-01.webp` | Foliage Sprites   | https://kenney.nl/assets/foliage-sprites |

The originals are 512×512 (Particle Pack) and 1024×1024 (Foliage Sprites) PNGs. Here they are
downscaled to 128×128 WebP; the foliage silhouettes are cropped to their shape and padded to a
square. They are white silhouettes/glows, tinted at draw time with `mask-image` (effects) or an SVG
`<mask>` (banners).
